Plan your next gameweeks

Load your team with its ID, then press Optimise transfers. The solver looks several gameweeks ahead and picks the transfers, starting XI and captain with the highest expected points. It accounts for free transfers you can roll, −4 hits, your bank and real selling prices, the 3-per-club rule and any chips you plan.

  • Projections: expected points for every player and gameweek, from recent xG/xA, minutes, defensive contributions, saves and bonus, adjusted for each opponent's attack and defence.
  • Override minutes on the Projections tab when you know something the model doesn't (rotation, a return from injury).
  • Fixtures: a ticker of each team's expected goals and clean-sheet chances.

xMins: click to set your own expected minutes for a player. This feeds into projections and the planner. Hover a GW cell to see where the points come from.

Team ratings

Attack: goals scored relative to an average team. Defence: goals conceded relative to average (lower is better). Fitted to this season's xG and goals, weighted towards recent matches, with schedule strength taken into account.
